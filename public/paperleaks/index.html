The paper leak protests

Paper leaks in India are no longer just an examination problem — they are a recurring trigger for street protests by students and job aspirants. In June–July 2024, the NEET-UG leak brought nationwide demonstrations, a Supreme Court-monitored CBI probe, the removal of the NTA's Director General and the first use of the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act. In December 2024 – January 2025, thousands of BPSC 70th-prelims aspirants held weeks of protests in Patna demanding a full re-exam; police responded with lathi charges and water cannon, and the dispute reached the Supreme Court. In Ranchi, JSSC-CGL candidates marched over 100 km and gheraoed the commission after the September 2024 re-exam was itself hit by leak allegations. Dehradun saw the Uttarakhand Unemployed Union lead protests until the UKSSSC graduate-level exam was cancelled in October 2025; in Odisha, aspirants gheraoed a minister's residence over the OSSSC CRE leaks; in Assam, student bodies demanded the education minister's resignation after every Class 11 paper was cancelled statewide in March 2025. The pattern behind the anger is visible in this tracker's data: only of incidents ended in a re-exam, while closed with no judgement at all — every incident and source is listed below.

Methodology

Base data: India Question Paper Leaks — Kaggle (66 incidents, Jun 2014–Aug 2024, CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0), with state-name typos cleaned. 54 further incidents (Jan 2024 – Jul 2026) were researched from credible outlets — The Indian Express, The Hindu, ThePrint/PTI, Deccan Herald, LiveLaw, Careers360, India TV, regional dailies and official board advisories — each row carrying its citation. "Accused" incidents include officially denied fake-leak scams, kept deliberately: they are part of the leak economy. Multi-day episodes of a single leak are counted once. Candidate counts are as reported (registered or appeared) and exist for incidents; totals are therefore minimums. Category, education level and outcome are derived by a committed, re-runnable script (scripts/paperleaks/generate_data.py) so the CSV and this page can never silently diverge.
